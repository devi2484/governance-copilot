Access Control Policy

All employee laptops must be encrypted using full-disk encryption and must automatically lock after 10 minutes of inactivity. This applies to all company-owned devices.

We conduct background verification checks on all new hires before their start date, in line with role sensitivity. Verification records are retained by HR for the duration of employment.

Users may submit a request to access a copy of the personal data we hold about them at any time, and we will respond within 30 days.
